Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 2026

Your privacy matters. This policy explains what information we collect when you visit SandraToyota.com or contact us, how we use it, and how we protect it.

Who We Are

This website is operated by Sandra Molina's team at Toyota of Hollywood, located at 1841 N State Road 7, Hollywood, FL 33021. References to "we," "us," or "our" in this policy refer to Sandra Molina and her team.

For questions about this policy, contact us at (786) 305-6671.

Information We Collect

We collect information you provide directly to us, including when you:

  • Submit a contact or scheduling form on this website
  • Call or text Sandra directly
  • Interact with our chat widget
  • Request information about a specific vehicle

This information may include your name, phone number, email address, and details about your vehicle inquiry.

We also collect certain technical information automatically, including your browser type, device type, IP address, and pages visited. This is standard web analytics data collected through cookies and similar technologies.

How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Respond to your inquiries and schedule appointments
  • Send you vehicle pricing, availability, and deal information you requested
  • Follow up on your interest in purchasing or leasing a Toyota
  • Improve the content and usability of this website

We do not sell your personal information to third parties. We do not share your information with unrelated businesses for their own marketing purposes.

Third-Party Tools

This website uses the following third-party services, which may collect data according to their own privacy policies:

  • GoHighLevel (LeadConnector) — used for our chat widget, appointment scheduling, and CRM. Their privacy policy can be found at gohighlevel.com.
  • Google Fonts — used to load website typography. Google may collect limited technical data when fonts are loaded.
  • Google Analytics (if applicable) — used to understand how visitors use this site.

Cookies

This website uses cookies to remember your preferences and understand how visitors use the site. Cookies are small text files placed on your device. You can control cookies through your browser settings. Disabling cookies may affect some functionality of this site.

Data Retention

We retain your contact information for as long as is necessary to fulfill the purpose for which it was collected — typically for the duration of an active sales inquiry — unless a longer retention period is required or permitted by law.

Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Request access to the personal information we hold about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate information
  • Request that we stop contacting you at any time

To exercise any of these rights, contact Sandra's team at (786) 305-6671.

Children's Privacy

This website is not directed to children under 13 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe we have inadvertently collected such information, please contact us immediately.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will update the "last updated" date at the top of this page. We encourage you to review this policy periodically.
